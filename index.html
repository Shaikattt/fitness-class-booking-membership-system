01 · Project overview

One simple system.
Better studio operations.

Independent gyms often rely on paper sign-in sheets, spreadsheets, or messaging apps to manage classes. FitnessFlow brings bookings, attendance, and membership status together in one focused web application.

01

No more overbooking

Real-time capacity rules protect every class from unsafe double-booking.

02

One source of truth

Centralised booking and attendance history for members and administrators.

03

Membership clarity

Expiry checks ensure only active members can reserve a class.

02 · Core functionality

Designed around
real people.

Every feature has a clear purpose: helping members stay active and helping administrators stay in control.

01

Member experience

Register, browse the timetable, book or cancel a place, review attendance, and manage membership status.

  • Class browsing and booking
  • Booking & attendance history
  • Membership renewal view
02

Admin control

Manage the operational details that keep a busy studio running smoothly.

  • Schedule and session management
  • Instructor assignment
  • Capacity and attendance reports
03

Secure by design

Business rules are enforced on the server, not just in the browser.

  • Hashed passwords and sessions
  • PDO prepared statements
  • Server-side validation
